How the Control Center works

A simple, transparent pipeline from camera to signal.

STEP 1 · DETECT

YOLOv8 counts vehicles

A pre-trained YOLOv8n model counts cars, motorcycles, buses and trucks in four zones of the traffic video.

STEP 2 · DECIDE

Rule-based signal logic

Each road gets a priority score from vehicle count and waiting time. The highest score gets green, for 15 to 45 seconds. This is a fixed rule set, not a trained prediction model.

STEP 3 · RESPOND

Simulation and emergency override

The 3D intersection follows the signal decision. An emergency vehicle can override the cycle for 30 seconds.

About the Route Finder: routes come from OpenStreetMap data and travel times are free-flow estimates. CITINOVA AI is not connected to a live traffic provider, so real-time congestion is not shown on commuter routes.
